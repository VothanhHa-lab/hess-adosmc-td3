\section{Simulation Results and Discussion}\label{sec:results}
This section first describes the simulation setup (Section~\ref{sec:setup}). It then reports, in order:
\begin{itemize}
\item the tier-1 comparison of A-DO-SMC with PI, fixed-gain DO-SMC and robust MPC (Section~\ref{sec:res_tier1});
\item the training, held-out test, safety-filter ablation, results of tier 2 on additional cycles, losses, storage sizing and computational cost (Sections~\ref{sec:res_train}--\ref{sec:res_cost}).
\end{itemize}
It closes with a discussion of mechanisms, trade-offs and limits (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}).
